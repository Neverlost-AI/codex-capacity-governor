Keep the tasks sequential on the same measured account—never overlap them or perform unrelated capacity-consuming work during an attempt. Confirm the starter manifest, dependency lock, exact brief and acceptance-case list **before** the Governor preflight.

For each task, the operator defines coherent work items for the full pinned scope, obtains a current authoritative required-bucket set, saves a manual preflight, reviews the exact evidence, explicitly confirms it, and records the policy result. A restrictive result is not execution permission. If the task cannot fit a safe bounded run, stop for a new founder scope decision rather than silently trimming requirements. If execution begins, link one governed run to that saved evaluation and record the explicit outcome, validation, adherence, failures/deferred work and supported actuals. Reopen the saved original and any amendment. An unfinished or interrupted task remains an honest `PARTIAL` or `FAILED` record, with factual reason and unknown usage left unknown.

## 4. Proposed per-attempt operating ceiling and measurement isolation

**Founder approval still required for the ceiling. It is a spending/operational stop rule, not an accuracy allowance or Governor policy constant.** Proposed ceiling: stop the attempt at the earlier of **180 active minutes** or **1,500 basis points of observed consumption in any single required bucket/reset cycle**, including all coding, tests, corrections and final verification. Do not borrow headroom from another window, convert plans, or reset the timer or budget by changing phases. A new reset cycle requires a fresh preflight and a separately recorded continuation decision.

1. Immediately before work, record a source reading for **every required bucket**: provider, window, reset cycle, displayed amount/unit, `USED` or `REMAINING` basis, precision, source observation time and operator capture time. Target capture within five minutes of the actual boundary; this is **not** a revision of Gate A's 30-minute freshness rule. If a necessary reading is missing, stale, reset-crossing or not comparable, do not begin attribution.
2. Record active start/pause/resume/stop times. Read every required bucket at phase boundaries, handoffs, at most every 30 active minutes and when nearing either ceiling. Increase check frequency when displayed headroom narrows; if display delay or precision cannot establish that the next work segment stays inside the proposed ceiling, stop early. Manual polling is not a provider-enforced hard cap and cannot retroactively prevent an unobserved crossing.
3. Within each **same** bucket/reset/plan limit, derive a whole-run delta from compatible start/end `USED` readings (end minus start) or `REMAINING` readings (start minus end). Preserve raw snapshots and precision. Never sum or substitute different windows. Readings around the entire attempt include review and validation. To claim an **implementation-only** actual, separately bracket each implementation segment and exclude review/validation; otherwise retain the whole-run observation but mark the implementation comparison `UNAVAILABLE`.
4. A reset, changed limit, unrelated account activity, unknown consumption, interruption, changed model/tools, or unreadable source breaks simple before/after attribution. Record the event and raw evidence. Pause, stop or re-preflight as required; do not guess the missing delta or turn it into zero. If the task ends incomplete, save the factually accurate existing outcome plus a reasoned note and any deferred work. A non-executed saved preflight is not a run.

Use the [blank attempt worksheet](dogfooding/T007_ATTEMPT_WORKSHEET_DRAFT.md) once per task. It separates **app-recorded facts**, **manual observations**, **derived comparisons** and **unavailable measurements**. Whole-run and implementation-only readings have distinct rows; every derived number cites compatible raw readings and the original issued forecast. Keep the original worksheet and append corrections with actor/time/reason. Private prompts, tokens, credentials and whole transcripts do not belong in the packet.
