## Accepted T7-2 measurement-isolation rules — protocol only

These are founder-accepted **protocol** rules for trustworthy manual attribution, not new Gate A freshness, provider conversion or product policy. Their application to each named task still needs the frozen starter, profile and budget in an execution grant.

1. **Freeze identity before work:** record task ID/scope and baseline commit, one anonymized measured account, displayed plan/tier and add-ons, model/version and reasoning settings when exposed, client/tool version, local Governor build/configuration, required bucket/window/reset IDs, source units/precision and the exact saved evaluation. A changed task, bucket set or execution attempt gets a new reviewed evaluation under Decision 0006; a changed configuration starts a separately labeled evidence cohort.
2. **One account, one active attempt:** no unrelated Codex work or parallel agents on that measured account from the starting reading through the ending reading. Pause coordinator work that would consume the same account while a delegated builder/reviewer works. Sequential handoffs, ordinary fixes and tests remain one bounded attempt. Log any human intervention or unavoidable external activity rather than silently treating it as task usage.
3. **Boundary readings:** target a manual reading for **every required window within five minutes before execution and within five minutes after the last in-workflow verification**. Record the displayed value/unit, used-versus-remaining basis, operator observation time, source timestamp and refresh delay if shown. This proposed five-minute capture rule is a measurement-protocol limit, **not** a change to Gate A's 30-minute snapshot freshness rule. If a reading misses the capture window or source delay makes its coverage uncertain, retain it but mark the affected before/after attribution `UNAVAILABLE`.
4. **Fresh authorization separately:** the saved preflight must satisfy Gate A's accepted time and known-post-observation-activity freshness rules at evaluation. Known capacity-consuming activity after a preflight observation invalidates that observation immediately. Do not call an old result current authorization because it belongs to the same run; obtain a new preflight when the accepted policy requires it.
5. **Comparable deltas only:** subtract before/after readings only when provider, window, reset cycle, amount basis (`used` versus `remaining`), limit and precision are compatible and no reset, allowance change or unrelated consuming activity confounds the interval. A used-capacity increase is not interchangeable with a remaining-capacity decrease without an approved, evidenced mapping. Record a reset or breach and mark affected usage `UNAVAILABLE`, never zero or an invented allocation. Keep each bucket independent.
6. **Phase-specific evidence:** a reading before the attempt and one after its final verification measure **whole-run** usage, including review, correction and validation; they cannot by themselves establish implementation-only actuals. Implementation-only comparison requires distinct compatible readings immediately before and after the implementation phase, with review and validation outside that interval. If work alternates phases, capture a separate pair for each implementation segment or leave implementation actual `UNAVAILABLE`; never subtract review or validation from a whole-run total by guess. Timestamp and source every implementation, correction and validation boundary that can actually be isolated. Assign usage to a category only if the source readings isolate that phase; otherwise retain supportable whole-run observation separately and leave the implementation-only forecast comparison unavailable. Do not derive category amounts merely to make them sum to a before/after delta. Record interruptions and known account activity with time, window and cause.
7. **No silent exclusions:** preserve all three attempted runs and all raw observations, including incomplete, failed, contaminated or reset-crossing cases. The worksheet names each unavailable measure and reason; it never removes the run from the operational sample. Keep secrets, private prompts and customer data out of shared evidence.

## Accepted T7-5 evidence pass / hold / fail gate — separate hosted and release gates remain

**PASS means the accepted three-attempt evidence floor is met and ready for a separate founder release decision; it is not itself release approval.** This gate was originally framed for local-only release and is retained without claiming that it proves hosted security or authorizes public publication. Require all of the following:

1. The exact fixed deployed build and hosted authentication/persistence/isolation/migration/startup/browser path are verified; the previously accepted local path remains tested. Lint, typecheck, full tests, coverage run, build, E2E, migration generation/check and `git diff --check` have recorded results without a functional failure. Record `pnpm format:check` honestly; a known checkout-line-ending failure is an exception only if canonical committed files are independently verified formatted and the founder explicitly accepts that documented exception. Never relabel a failed command as passed.
2. **Three of three** distinct real bounded **executed** attempts have a saved pre-work evaluation, linked governed run, original issued evidence, explicit outcome/validation/adherence, source-classed worksheet, and successful reopen of every saved version. If an actual amendment occurred, its predecessor and reason reopen; the controlled amendment rehearsal must pass even if no real amendment was needed. A failed development task can count if its evidence is intact and its outcome is truthful. A non-executed restrictive preflight is retained as separate evidence, never counted as one of the three or treated as permission to work.
3. **At least two of the three** real attempts have compatible `COMPLETED` implementation actuals for every bucket that each attempt required, with source, same-cycle attribution and reproducible comparison. The third may be partial, failed or unavailable with an explicit reason. This is a proposed measurement-repeatability floor, **not** an accuracy target. If fewer than two qualify, keep all attempts, improve collection and repeat with an additional real attempt rather than manufacturing data.
4. The separately labeled rehearsal matrix passes all listed critical paths: partial/failed lower bounds, unavailable comparison, append-only amendment, saved restrictive result without false permission, legacy `UNGUIDED` history and reopen. Rehearsals never count toward the three real attempts or the two comparable outcomes.
5. For **each** real attempt, the operator can use the visible UI—without inspecting raw JSON—to identify the decision at evaluation time, limiting window, protected reserves, supported next action and comparison/uncertainty limits. Record any wrong or confusing answer and the exact screen/label. There is no unresolved task-blocking usability defect or missing-record problem.
6. The founder receives individual cases and counts/denominators, all exclusions and negative evidence, technical verification, the worksheet/source provenance, privacy-safe defect log and a recommendation. Do not claim measured savings, predictive accuracy, productivity improvement, voluntary retention or multi-plan generality from this sample. The 12-run benchmark, other plans and an invited pilot are **not** pass criteria.

**HOLD (not release-ready):** fewer than three complete operational records; fewer than two compatible completed comparisons; a missed reading or unresolved confounder that is falsely used for attribution, compromises either of the two required comparable attempts, or makes a record untrustworthy; a required test or local startup failure; an unaccepted formatting exception; unresolved task-blocking confusion; incomplete rehearsal evidence; or an unknown risk needing investigation. An honestly retained third attempt with an explicitly `UNAVAILABLE` comparison due to a missed reading or confounder **does not by itself trigger HOLD** when the other two satisfy the compatible-completed requirement and the third operational record is complete. Preserve all data and propose a bounded correction or another measured attempt where a gate is unmet. A naturally partial/failed run is not itself a product failure.

**FAIL / stop the affected protocol:** any false work-authorization display, unauthorized hosted or local access, cross-account/private-demo data exposure, lost/rewritten original or amendment evidence, cross-bucket borrowing/conversion, fabricated capacity attribution, secret leakage, or a defect that makes the record untrustworthy. Do not patch evidence in place or continue measuring on that build. A separately approved fix and fresh verification are required before resuming. A founder must still decide whether a later corrected build and new cohort warrant release.
